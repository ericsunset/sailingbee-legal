SailingBee

Community Guidelines

Last updated: November 2026

SailingBee works because sailors trust each other. These guidelines exist to keep that trust intact — on the water and off it.

The basics

Be respectful Treat everyone the way you'd want to be treated aboard. Disagreements happen — handle them like adults. Harassment, threats, or discriminatory language of any kind will result in removal.
Be honest Your profile should represent you accurately. Don't misrepresent your experience level, certifications, or intentions. Fake profiles undermine the whole community.
Be clear about expectations Whether you're a skipper posting a sail or crew joining a sail, communicate clearly about what the trip involves — duration, conditions, experience required, costs if any.
Keep it sailing SailingBee is for connecting sailors, not for soliciting unrelated business, selling products, or spamming other users. Keep your posts and messages on topic.
Respect privacy Don't share other users' personal information without their permission. What's shared in messages stays in messages.

Consequences

We review every report. Depending on severity, violations may result in a warning, temporary suspension, or permanent removal. We make these calls judgment by judgment — there's no automated system.

How to report

Use the Help & support form in the app (Profile → Account → Help & support → Report a post or user). You can also block a user from their post or from chat. We read every report, usually within a day or two.
