This page is a plain-English summary of important limitations. It is part of our Terms of Service and should be read alongside it.

SailingBee is a matchmaking platform

SailingBee connects crew with skippers. That's it. We are not a charter company, a boat rental service, a maritime employer, or a crewing agency. We are a technology platform.

We are not a party to any sail arranged through the app. We have no involvement in, and no control over, what happens once a crew and skipper agree to sail together.

We do not vet skippers or vessels

SailingBee does not verify skippers' certifications, licenses, insurance coverage, or the seaworthiness of any vessel. The information on a skipper's profile is self-reported.

Before you board any vessel, you are responsible for:

  • Verifying the skipper's qualifications and certifications directly
  • Confirming that appropriate marine insurance is in place
  • Satisfying yourself that the vessel is seaworthy and properly equipped
  • Understanding the trip conditions and your own ability to participate safely

Rental and charter boats

Some skippers arrange sails on chartered or rented vessels. SailingBee has no relationship with boat owners, marinas, or charter operators. Any agreements, liabilities, or disputes related to the vessel are between the skipper and the vessel's owner — not SailingBee.

Limitation of liability

To the fullest extent permitted by law, SailingBee is not liable for:

  • On-water incidents, injuries, illness, or death
  • Damage to or loss of property
  • Disputes between crew members and skippers
  • Any harm arising from a sail arranged through the app

You use SailingBee and participate in any sail at your own risk.
