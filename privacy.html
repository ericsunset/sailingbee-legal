Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 2026  ·  Last updated: May 2026

We built SailingBee for sailors, not for data collection. Here's exactly what we collect, why, and how you can control it.

1. What we collect

DataWhy we collect it
Email addressAccount authentication and service communications
Display nameShown to other users on your profile and posts
Experience levelShown on your profile to help skippers evaluate crew
BioOptional — shown on your profile
Profile photoOptional — shown on your profile
Posts and messagesCore app functionality
Device push tokenSending you notifications you've opted into

We do not collect location data, contacts, or any data beyond what's listed above.

2. How we use your data

  • To operate and improve the SailingBee app
  • To connect crew with skippers
  • To send you notifications you've enabled
  • To investigate safety or abuse reports

We do not sell your data. We do not use it for advertising.

3. Third-party services

  • Supabase — database, file storage, and authentication infrastructure
  • Sentry — crash reporting and error monitoring

Both services are used under data processing agreements consistent with applicable privacy law.

4. Data retention

We keep your data as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, your profile, posts, and messages are permanently deleted. This process may take up to 30 days to complete across all systems.

5. Your rights

You can request a copy of your data, correction of inaccurate data, or deletion of your account at any time. Use the Help & Support form in the app or email hello@sailingbee.app.

If you are in the EU or UK, you have additional rights under GDPR. If you are in California, you have additional rights under CCPA. Contact us to exercise any of these rights.

6. Changes to this policy

We'll notify you by email before any material changes take effect.

7. Contact

Email us at hello@sailingbee.app.
