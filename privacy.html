2. Location

SailingBee asks for your location only when you turn on "Use my location" in the Browse filters. If you do not allow location access, the app still works, and you can search by harbor or place instead.

When you allow it, the app reads your approximate location (to about 100 meters) one time, while you use the app. It does not track your location in the background. The app sends that location to our server with your search, so that the server can find and sort sails near you. We do not save it to your account or our database.

The location stays in the app's memory for your current search only. Like any web request, it can appear for a short time in our hosting provider's request logs and in crash and performance reports. Those records are deleted on the provider's schedule. You can turn off location access at any time in the iPhone Settings app.

The harbor or place that you choose for a post is part of the post, and other users see it.

3. What other people can see

All sails are public. Any signed-in user can see a post, the skipper's name and photo, and the names and photos of the crew who joined. Signed-in users can also see your experience level and bio.

Anyone with a sail's share link can open a web page that shows the post, the skipper's name and photo, and the crew's names and photos, without signing in. Your experience level and bio are not on that page.

Only the skipper and crew of a sail can read its group chat.

4. How we use your data

  • To run the SailingBee app and connect skippers with crew
  • To send you the notifications and emails that the app needs, such as sign-in codes, join updates, and chat messages
  • To keep the app safe, investigate reports, and stop abuse
  • To find and fix crashes and errors

We do not sell your data. We do not share it for advertising, and we do not use it for advertising.

6. Deleting your account

You can delete your account in the app, under Profile > Account > Delete account. The deletion starts at once, and you cannot undo it.

  • We delete your profile (email address, display name, experience level, bio, and photo), your sign-in account, your device push tokens, your blocks, and your notifications.
  • Upcoming sails that you host are cancelled, and their crew is notified. You are removed from upcoming sails that you joined, and their skippers are notified.
  • Past sails that you hosted or joined, and the chat messages that you sent, stay for the other sailors. They show a deleted-account name in place of your name, and they are no longer linked to you.
  • We remove the text and reply email of your support requests, and keep only a record that a request was made.

Most of this happens in seconds. If a step fails, for example the removal of your photo from storage, our system completes it automatically, usually within a few minutes. Copies in our service providers' logs, crash reports, and backups are deleted on their own schedules.

If you cannot use the app, ask us to delete your account through the contact details below.

7. Data retention

We keep your data while your account is active. You can edit your profile at any time. Section 6 describes what happens when you delete your account. We keep a record of reports and the actions we took on them for as long as we need it to keep the app safe.

8. Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data or the correction of incorrect data, and you can delete your account at any time. To make a request, use the Help & support form in the app or email hello@sailingbee.us.

If you live in California, you can also ask what personal information we have collected about you and how we use it, and we will not treat you differently for making any of these requests. We do not sell or share your personal information as California law defines those terms.

Do Not Track: we do not track you across other apps or websites, so a Do Not Track signal from your browser or device does not change anything we do.

9. Age

SailingBee is for people who are 18 or older. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you think that a person under 18 has an account, contact us and we will delete it.
